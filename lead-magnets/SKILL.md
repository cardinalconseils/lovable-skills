# Lead Magnets

Expert knowledge for creating, designing, and optimizing lead magnets that attract high-quality leads.

## What Makes a Good Lead Magnet

1. **Specific:** Solves one specific problem, not everything
2. **Instantly valuable:** Value felt immediately, not over time
3. **Positioned for sale:** The person who wants this is exactly the person who should buy your product

## Lead Magnet Type Selection

| Type | Best for | Lead quality |
|---|---|---|
| Checklist / cheat sheet | Specific process, quick reference | Medium |
| Template | Recurring task, saves time | High |
| Calculator / tool | ROI or sizing decisions | Very high |
| Mini-course (email) | Complex topic, education | High |
| Report / research | Industry authority building | Medium |
| Swipe file | Examples and inspiration | Medium |
| Webinar / workshop | Live education, warm nurture | High |
| Audit / assessment | Diagnostic, high-touch | Very high |

**Type selection rules:**
- Buyer in evaluation mode → calculator or comparison template
- Buyer in education mode → guide or mini-course
- Buyer has a recurring operational task → template or checklist
- Trying to segment by pain point → assessment

## Value-to-Ask Ratio

- Checklist or cheat sheet → Email only
- Template or calculator → Email + name
- Report or mini-course → Email + name + company
- Audit or 1:1 consultation → Full form

**Rule:** Only ask for information you will actually use in follow-up.

## Lead Magnet Creation Process

1. Define ICP and trigger moment (who, what they're trying to do, what specific problem this solves)
2. Write the before/after promise (this is your landing page headline)
3. Create the content

## Landing Page Copy

**Headline formula:** "[Get/Download/Access] [Specific resource] to [Specific outcome] [Time qualifier or without sacrifice]"

**Body copy structure:**
1. What you get (2-3 specific bullets)
2. Who this is for (one line)
3. Social proof
4. Email form + CTA

## Confirmation Email Sequence

**Email 1 (immediate):** Deliver the lead magnet.

**Email 2 (Day 1-2):** "The one thing most people miss in [topic]" — value amplifier.

**Email 3 (Day 3-4):** Mini case study bridging from the resource to your product.

## Common Rationalizations

| Rationalization | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Any lead magnet is better than none" | A mispositioned lead magnet fills your list with non-buyers. |
| "Make it comprehensive" | Comprehensive guides are content, not lead magnets. Lead magnets solve one specific problem immediately. |
| "We'll build the sequence later" | 70% of subscribers disengage within 72 hours. The immediate sequence converts them. |
| "Everyone will want this resource" | "Everyone" means "no one." The more specific the ICP, the higher the conversion rate. |

## Verification

- [ ] ICP and trigger moment defined before creation
- [ ] Before/after promise written and used as headline
- [ ] Lead magnet type matched to buyer stage
- [ ] Form fields match friction-to-value ratio
- [ ] Landing page: specific headline, 3 concrete bullets, social proof, single CTA
- [ ] Thank you page delivers value and offers a next step
- [ ] 3-email delivery sequence configured
- [ ] Activated lead rate tracking configured (action within 30 days)
