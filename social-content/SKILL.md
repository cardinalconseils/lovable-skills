# Social Content

Expertise in building social content that grows audiences and converts them. Every post has a job (educate, prove, humanize, convert). Content without a job is noise.

## Platform Selection by Audience

- **Twitter/X:** Founder voice, B2B thought leadership. Threads > single tweets. 280 char limit.
- **LinkedIn:** B2B sales, hiring, investor visibility. 210 chars above fold before "see more." Up to 3,000 chars.
- **Instagram:** Visual products, B2C. Reels > static for reach. Caption up to 2,200 chars.
- **TikTok/Reels:** Educational entertainment. 60 seconds ideal. Authentic beats produced.

**Simple rule:** B2B = LinkedIn + Twitter/X. B2C = Instagram + TikTok/Reels. Don't spread across all four without a full content team.

## Content Pillar Ratios (30-Day Calendar)

| Pillar | Ratio | Posts | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Education | 40% | 12/month | Teach something useful. Build authority. |
| Proof | 20% | 6/month | Case studies, results, testimonials, before/after. |
| Culture | 20% | 6/month | Team, process, behind-the-scenes. Builds liking. |
| Engagement | 10% | 3/month | Questions, polls, opinion posts. |
| Promotion | 10% | 3/month | Direct product mention, offer, or CTA. |

Over-promoting (> 10%) → audience disengages. Under-promoting (0%) → lots of engagement, no revenue.

## Hook Writing

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Curiosity | "Nobody talks about this, but [insight]" |
| List | "7 things I learned building [X] to [milestone]:" |
| Contrarian | "Hot take: [thing everyone does] is actually killing your [outcome]" |
| Story | "I made a $50K mistake last year. Here's what happened:" |
| Question | "What would you do if your best customer churned tomorrow?" |
| Outcome | "We went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]. Here's how:" |

**Hooks that kill posts:** "In today's post...", "I wanted to share...", "Excited to announce...", starting with "We".

## Platform-Specific Best Practices

**LinkedIn:** First 210 chars must stand alone. Line breaks every 2-3 sentences. End with a question. Best time: Tue-Thu 7-9am or 12-1pm.

**Twitter/X:** Threads outperform single tweets. Thread format: Hook → 5-10 insight tweets → CTA tweet. Reply to comments within first hour.

**Instagram:** Carousel posts get 3× more reach than static. Reels rewatched = algorithm boost. 5-10 relevant hashtags.

**TikTok/Reels:** First 3 seconds must hook. Add text captions (60%+ views without sound). Pattern: hook → tension → payoff.

## Content Templates

### Education Post
```
[Hook — contrarian claim or outcome statement]

Here's the framework I use:

1. [First insight]
2. [Second insight]
3. [Key unlock]

The mistake most people make: [wrong approach]
The fix: [your approach]

[Question or CTA]
```

### Social Proof Post
```
[Customer type] went from [before] to [after].

Here's what changed:
[The specific thing that made the difference]

[Quote or metric]
[Low-friction CTA]
```

## Common Rationalizations

| Rationalization | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We post when we have something to say" | Algorithms reward consistency. Irregular posting gets deprioritized. |
| "Our content gets engagement but no sales" | Missing the 10% promotion pillar. Add it. |
| "LinkedIn is too formal for our brand" | The most viral LinkedIn content reads like a personal story, not a press release. |
| "We need better design before we post" | Text posts on LinkedIn outperform graphics for B2B. |
| "We need to be on all platforms" | One platform done well beats four done poorly. |
