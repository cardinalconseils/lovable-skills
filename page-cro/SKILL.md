# Page Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO)

Expert knowledge for diagnosing, testing, and improving conversion rates across marketing pages.

## CRO Mindset

CRO is not about making pages prettier. It's about removing the reasons people don't convert. Start with research, not redesign.

## Conversion Research Stack

**Quantitative (what is happening):**
- GA4: Funnel visualization, scroll depth, device breakdown, traffic source by conversion rate
- Heatmaps: Click maps, scroll maps
- Session recordings: Hotjar, FullStory, Microsoft Clarity (free)

**Qualitative (why it's happening):**
- On-page surveys: "What stopped you from signing up today?"
- Exit intent surveys
- User testing: 5 users completing a task reveals ~80% of usability issues

## Above-the-Fold Optimization

The first screen must communicate:
1. What you do (one sentence — no jargon)
2. Who it's for (ICP named explicitly)
3. Why it matters (key outcome)
4. What to do next (one clear CTA)

**Hero copy formula:**
```
H1: [Specific outcome] for [ICP]
Subheadline: [How you deliver it] — [key differentiator]
CTA: [Specific action] — [friction reducer]
```

**5-second test:** Show your above-the-fold to someone unfamiliar for 5 seconds. Ask: "What does this company do? Who is it for?" If they can't answer, rewrite.

## Trust Signals by Position

**Above the fold:** Logo, customer logos, review rating, user count.

**Mid-page:** Testimonials (specific outcome + full name + company + photo), case study snippets.

**Near CTA:** Security badges, money-back guarantee, "No credit card required", privacy signal.

**Footer:** Contact information, security certifications, press mentions.

## Friction Removal

| Friction | Fix |
|---|---|
| Long signup form | Reduce to email only |
| Email verification before product access | Move to after first use |
| Credit card required for free trial | Remove if model allows |
| Complex pricing page | Add "Most teams choose X" recommendation |
| Navigation with too many options | Reduce nav on landing pages |

## CTA Optimization

- Primary CTA visible without scrolling on desktop and mobile
- Repeat CTA every 2-3 scrolls on long pages
- Start with a verb, be specific about what happens next
- Add friction reducer immediately below ("No credit card required")
- High contrast, 44px minimum touch target on mobile

## Social Proof Placement

| Position | Best proof type |
|---|---|
| Hero section | Customer count, logo bar, review rating |
| Problem section | Quote acknowledging the pain |
| Solution section | Before/after, feature-specific testimonial |
| Near CTA | Short outcome-specific quote with name and photo |

**Testimonial quality:** "We reduced onboarding time from 6 hours to 45 minutes" beats "Great product. Highly recommend."

## Pricing Page Optimization

- Clear tier names (outcome-oriented, not just Basic/Pro/Enterprise)
- Recommended plan highlighted
- Annual/monthly toggle with annual discount shown
- FAQ addressing pricing objections
- Anchoring: show highest tier first

## Common Rationalizations

| Rationalization | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We need a full redesign to fix conversions" | Most conversion lifts come from copy changes and friction removal, not redesigns. |
| "Our industry has naturally low conversion rates" | Industry benchmarks explain your current rate, not your ceiling. |
| "More features on the page means more reasons to convert" | More choices create decision paralysis. |
| "The design looks great" | Aesthetics affect trust, not clarity. A beautiful page with a confusing value prop still doesn't convert. |
