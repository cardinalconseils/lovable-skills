# Launch Strategy

Expertise in building launch campaigns that match the product's actual maturity stage.

## Maturity Stage Adaptation

**Prototype:** HN Show HN and relevant subreddits. No paid ads or press. Goal: 10-20 honest user conversations.

**Pilot:** Product Hunt viable if building community for 90 days. Email list is your most reliable channel. Goal: 25-50 power users.

**Candidate:** Press, paid ads, and partner channels worth the effort now. Press requires 4 weeks lead time. Goal: measurable top-of-funnel with conversion to paid.

**Production:** Ongoing demand generation, not a launch event.

## 8-Week Launch Timeline

### Weeks -8 to -4: Build the Audience

Pre-launch converts at 30-40%. Day-of cold traffic at 2-5%. Build the waitlist.

- Launch a waitlist landing page
- Post daily on LinkedIn and Twitter/X about the problem (not the product)
- Identify 20-30 potential "super users"
- If targeting Product Hunt: create account, engage before you need it

### Weeks -4 to -2: Warm the Audience

- Send 2-3 teaser emails to waitlist
- Build social proof: beta user quotes, early metrics
- Brief 3-5 media contacts if Candidate stage (personal emails with embargo)
- Prepare Product Hunt assets if relevant

### Week -1: Final Preparation

- [ ] Product demo video recorded and published
- [ ] Landing page finalized with social proof
- [ ] Email announcement written and ready
- [ ] Social posts queued
- [ ] Support ready for launch-day questions
- [ ] Analytics in place

### Launch Day

1. Email blast to waitlist at 8am audience timezone
2. Post on LinkedIn + Twitter/X with the story, not the pitch
3. If Product Hunt: go live at 12:01am PST
4. Post in relevant communities — only where you've been active
5. Personal outreach to 10-20 contacts

### Week +1: Capitalize on Momentum

- Send "thank you + results" email to waitlist
- Reach out to everyone who signed up but hasn't activated
- Write a "lessons learned from launch" post

## Product Hunt Strategy

- 90 days of community building before launch day
- Hunter outreach 2 weeks before
- Target 200 upvotes in first 24 hours
- Go live at 12:01am PST

**Assets:** Tagline (60 chars, specific outcome), Description (260 chars), Gallery (4-6 screenshots), First comment (founder story), Demo video (60-90 seconds).

## Common Rationalizations

| Rationalization | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We'll launch when the product is perfect" | Perfect is a moving target. Launch at Pilot stage — users tell you what perfect actually means. |
| "We don't need press — our community is enough" | For Candidate stage, press provides the credibility signal that converts skeptics. |
| "Product Hunt is pay-to-win" | The teams that win PH spent 90 days building relationships on the platform. That's community. |
| "We can compress the pre-launch to 2 weeks" | At Candidate stage, press lead time alone is 4 weeks. |
