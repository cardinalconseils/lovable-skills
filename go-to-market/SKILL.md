# Go-to-Market

Builds the launch plan and channel strategy that gets the product in front of the right people before the runway runs out. GTM is not a launch event — it's a repeatable motion from ICP to closed customer.

## GTM Foundation: Three Questions Before Any Tactic

1. **Who is the ICP?** Not a demographic — a behavioral profile. "B2B SaaS companies with 10–50 employees whose ops team manually reconciles data across 3+ tools every week."
2. **What is the trigger?** The specific event that makes them look for a solution right now. Not "always in pain" — what changed that made today the day they search?
3. **Where do they already look?** The community, publication, influencer, or search query they trust before they trust you.

No channel decision is valid until these three are answered.

## Channel Selection by Stage

| Stage | Best channels | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **Pre-launch (0 users)** | Direct outreach, founder network, waiting list | Speed of feedback, not scale |
| **Early traction (1–100 users)** | Community (Reddit, Slack groups, Discord), founder-led content | Reputation > reach |
| **Growth (100–1000 users)** | SEO content, Product Hunt, partnerships, referral | Compound, low CAC |
| **Scale (1000+ users)** | Paid acquisition, sales team, channel partners | Now you can afford CAC |

**Rule:** Don't run paid acquisition until you have a conversion rate worth paying to scale. Paid amplifies what's working — it doesn't create what isn't.

## ICP-to-Channel Fit Matrix

| ICP type | Highest-ROI channel |
|---|---|
| Developer | GitHub, Hacker News, dev Twitter/X, technical blog, open source |
| Operator / PMM | LinkedIn, Slack communities, newsletter sponsorships |
| SMB founder | Twitter/X, Facebook Groups, local networks, cold email |
| Enterprise buyer | LinkedIn outbound, analyst relations, partner ecosystem |
| Consumer | TikTok/Instagram, SEO, influencer, paid social |

## Pre-Launch Playbook (0 → First 100 Users)

**6 weeks out:**
- [ ] ICP defined in behavioral terms
- [ ] 50-person waitlist seeded from personal network
- [ ] One "founder story" piece published (why you built this)
- [ ] 3 ICP conversations completed — validate trigger and language

**3 weeks out:**
- [ ] Landing page live with specific ICP-targeted headline
- [ ] Waitlist incentive activated (early access, discount, founder tier)
- [ ] 5 pilot users confirmed for launch week
- [ ] 1 community thread seeded in highest-density ICP community

**Launch week:**
- [ ] Pilot users active — gather quotes and early results
- [ ] Product Hunt launch (Tuesday–Thursday, 12:01am PT)
- [ ] Founder post on LinkedIn + Twitter/X with the story
- [ ] Email waitlist with the announcement
- [ ] 10 direct 1:1 messages to highest-fit prospects

**Post-launch (week 2–4):**
- [ ] First 3 case studies drafted from pilot users
- [ ] Identify which channel drove highest-quality signups (not just volume)
- [ ] Double down on that channel, pause others

## Messaging Architecture

One message hierarchy, used consistently across all channels:

```
Positioning: [Product] is the [category] for [ICP] who [trigger].
Value prop:  We help [ICP] [achieve outcome] without [sacrifice].
Proof:       [Specific result] for [specific customer type].
CTA:         [Specific action] — [friction reducer].
```

All channels use the same positioning. Different formats — same message.

## Founder-Led Sales (Pre-Product-Market Fit)

Before PMF, the founder sells. Not the sales team — they don't exist yet, and even if they did, the founder needs to hear the objections directly.

**Founder sales motion:**
1. 10 cold outreach messages per week — personalized, 3 sentences, one ask
2. Every demo = discovery call first (listen 70%, talk 30%)
3. Every "no" gets a follow-up question: "What would have made this a yes?"
4. Close the feedback loop: what they say in demos updates the website copy

**When to hire sales:** When the founder can close > 50% of qualified demos, you have a repeatable pitch worth teaching.

## Metrics That Matter by Stage

| Stage | Metric to track |
|---|---|
| Pre-launch | Waitlist conversion rate, ICP interview completion |
| Early traction | Time-to-activation, week-1 retention, NPS from first 20 users |
| Growth | CAC, LTV, CAC payback period, referral rate |
| Scale | Net revenue retention, sales cycle length, win rate |

**Rule:** Don't track scale metrics at traction stage. CAC is meaningless when n=10.

## Common Rationalizations

| Rationalization | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We'll launch on Product Hunt and get users" | Product Hunt is an amplifier, not a source. Launched with 0 audience = 0 upvotes. |
| "We need to be on every channel" | You have one channel that will work. Find it first, then add the second. |
| "We'll figure out distribution after we build" | Distribution is harder than building. Start the GTM motion at the same time as engineering. |
| "Our product will grow virally" | Virality is a feature you design, not a force that happens. What's the referral mechanic? |
| "Paid ads will get us to PMF" | Paid ads scale a working motion. They don't create PMF. Don't pay to learn. |

## Verification

- [ ] ICP defined in behavioral terms (trigger, not demographic)
- [ ] Channel selected based on ICP-to-channel fit — not "what's trendy"
- [ ] Messaging hierarchy written: positioning + value prop + proof + CTA
- [ ] Pre-launch checklist completed (waitlist, pilots, community seeding)
- [ ] First 3 channels chosen — no more
- [ ] Founder-led sales motion active until PMF confirmed
- [ ] One GTM metric tracked per stage — not a dashboard of 20
