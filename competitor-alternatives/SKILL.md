# Competitor Alternative & Comparison Pages

Expert knowledge for creating high-converting competitor alternative pages that rank for "alternative to X" queries and win deals at the bottom of the funnel.

## Why These Pages Matter

"Alternative to [competitor]" and "[Your product] vs [competitor]" queries are among the highest-intent searches on the internet. Someone searching for alternatives has already decided they want to buy.

These pages serve two purposes:
1. **SEO:** Capture bottom-of-funnel search traffic
2. **Sales enablement:** Give prospects an honest, structured comparison to share internally

## Page Types

**Alternative page:** "Best [Category] alternatives to [Competitor]" — broader keyword coverage, you can be one of many alternatives listed.

**Vs page:** "[Your Product] vs [Competitor]" — head-to-head format, higher conversion when reader has narrowed to two options.

**Category landing page:** "Best [category software] in [year]" — earns backlinks from people sharing comparison guides.

## Page Structure: Alternative Page

1. **H1:** "Best [Competitor] Alternatives in [Year]"
2. **Why people look for alternatives** (100-150 words, objective framing)
3. **The alternatives** — for each: Best for, Pricing, Key strength, Key limitation
4. **Comparison table** — features where your product genuinely wins
5. **FAQ section** with schema markup

List 4-6 alternatives including yourself. If you list only yourself, it's an ad, not a page.

## Page Structure: Vs Page

1. **TL;DR Summary Box** — "[Your Product] is better for: X" / "[Competitor] is better for: Y"
2. **Side-by-side comparison table** — pricing, features, integrations, support, onboarding, security
3. **Fair assessment of competitor strengths**
4. **Switching section** — migration time, assistance available, data import, risk if it doesn't work out

## Fair Framing Principles

1. Never make false claims about the competitor's product
2. Acknowledge legitimate strengths
3. Use current pricing and features
4. Date the page ("Last updated: [Month Year]")

## SEO Optimization

- Include competitor brand name in: URL, H1, first paragraph, meta title, meta description
- Build internal links from high-traffic blog posts to the comparison page
- Keep the page updated — search engines and users reward freshness

## Common Rationalizations

| Rationalization | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We shouldn't mention competitors — it gives them free exposure" | Buyers are already searching for these comparisons. If you're not the answer, a competitor is. |
| "We'll just say we're better in every way" | Savvy buyers distrust one-sided comparisons. Honest assessments convert better. |
| "We don't want to drive traffic to a page that mentions competitors" | "Alternative to X" traffic is pre-qualified. These visitors have decided to buy. |
| "Our product is too complex to compare in a table" | A table with footnotes is always better than no comparison. |

## Verification

- [ ] Primary keyword researched and confirmed with search volume data
- [ ] Competitor information current (verified within 30 days)
- [ ] Page includes fair acknowledgment of competitor strengths
- [ ] Comparison table covers features where your product genuinely wins
- [ ] FAQ section with schema markup implemented
- [ ] Switching/migration section addresses cost and risk
- [ ] CTA placed above fold and after each major section
- [ ] Page dated with "last updated" marker
