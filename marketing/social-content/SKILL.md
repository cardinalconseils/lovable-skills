# Social Content

Expertise in building social content that grows audiences and converts them — not content that fills a calendar.

## Platform Selection by Audience

- **Twitter/X:** Founder voice, B2B thought leadership. 280 characters per post.
- **LinkedIn:** B2B sales, hiring, investor visibility. 210 chars above fold before "see more". Up to 3,000 characters.
- **Instagram:** Visual products, lifestyle brands, B2C. Caption up to 2,200 characters.
- **TikTok/Reels:** Educational entertainment. Hook in first 3 seconds. 60 seconds ideal.

**Simple rule:** B2B → LinkedIn + Twitter/X. B2C → Instagram + TikTok/Reels.

## Content Pillar Ratios (30-Day Calendar)

| Pillar | Ratio | Posts | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| Education | 40% | 12/month | Teach something useful. Build authority. |
| Proof | 20% | 6/month | Case studies, results, testimonials, before/after. |
| Culture | 20% | 6/month | Team, process, behind-the-scenes. Builds liking. |
| Engagement | 10% | 3/month | Questions, polls, opinion posts. Drives comments. |
| Promotion | 10% | 3/month | Direct product mention, offer, or CTA. |

Over-promoting (> 10%) → audience disengages. Under-promoting (0%) → engagement but no revenue.

## Hook Writing

| Formula | Example |
|---|---|
| Curiosity | "Nobody talks about this, but [insight]" |
| List | "7 things I learned building [X] to [milestone]:" |
| Contrarian | "Hot take: [thing everyone does] is actually killing your [outcome]" |
| Story | "I made a $50K mistake last year. Here's what happened:" |
| Question | "What would you do if your best customer churned tomorrow?" |
| Outcome | "We went from [before] to [after] in [timeframe]. Here's how:" |

**Hooks that kill posts:** "In today's post...", "I wanted to share...", "Excited to announce...", Starting with "We"

## Platform-Specific Best Practices

### LinkedIn
- First 210 characters must stand alone
- Line breaks between every 2-3 sentences
- End with a question to drive comments
- Best time: Tuesday-Thursday, 7-9am or 12-1pm
- Carousel PDFs generate high saves and shares

### Twitter/X
- Threads outperform single tweets
- Thread format: Hook tweet → 5-10 insight tweets → CTA tweet
- 1-2 hashtags max
- Reply to comments within first hour

### Instagram
- Carousel posts get 3× more reach than static posts
- Hashtags: 5-10 relevant ones, not 30 generic ones
- Stories maintain audience warmth; feed posts build audience

### TikTok/Reels
- First 3 seconds must hook — text overlay + movement
- Add text captions (60%+ of views are without sound)
- Pattern: hook → tension → payoff

## Content Templates

### Education Post
```
[Hook — contrarian claim or outcome statement]

Here's the framework I use:

1. [First insight]
2. [Second insight]
3. [Third insight]

The mistake most people make: [common wrong approach]

The fix: [your approach]

[Question or CTA]
```

### Social Proof Post
```
[Customer name or type] went from [specific before] to [specific after].

Here's what changed:
[The specific thing that made the difference]

[Metric or quote]

[Low-friction CTA]
```

## Common Rationalizations

| Rationalization | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We post when we have something to say" | Algorithms reward consistency. Irregular posting gets deprioritized. |
| "Our content gets engagement but no sales" | Missing the 10% promotion pillar. Add it. |
| "We need to be on all platforms" | One platform done well beats four platforms done poorly. |
| "We need better design before we post" | Text posts on LinkedIn outperform graphics. Words > design on most B2B platforms. |
