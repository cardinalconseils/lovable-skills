# Launch Strategy

Expertise in building launch campaigns that match the product's actual maturity stage.

## Maturity Stage Adaptation

**Prototype:** Hacker News (Show HN) and relevant subreddits. Do NOT run paid ads or press outreach. Goal: 10-20 honest user conversations.

**Pilot:** Product Hunt is viable if you've been building community for 90 days. Email list is your most reliable channel. Goal: 25-50 power users.

**Candidate:** Press, paid ads, and partner channels are worth the effort now. Press requires 4 weeks lead time minimum. Goal: measurable top-of-funnel with conversion to paid.

**Production:** Ongoing demand generation, not a launch event.

## 8-Week Launch Timeline

### Weeks -8 to -4: Build the Audience

Pre-launch converts at 30-40%. Day-of cold traffic converts at 2-5%.

- Launch a waitlist landing page
- Post daily about the problem you're solving (not the product)
- Identify 20-30 potential "super users"
- If targeting Product Hunt: create PH account, engage with community before you need it

### Weeks -4 to -2: Warm the Audience

- Send 2-3 teaser emails to waitlist
- Build social proof: beta user quotes, early metrics
- Brief 3-5 media contacts if Candidate stage
- Prepare Product Hunt assets if relevant

### Week -1: Final Preparation

- [ ] Product demo video recorded and published
- [ ] Landing page finalized with social proof from beta users
- [ ] Email announcement written and ready
- [ ] Social posts queued
- [ ] Analytics in place

### Launch Day Sequence

1. Email blast to waitlist at 8am audience timezone
2. Post on LinkedIn + Twitter/X with the story, not the pitch
3. If Product Hunt: go live at 12:01am PST
4. Post in relevant communities (only where you've been active)
5. Personal outreach to 10-20 contacts

## Product Hunt Strategy

**Requirements:**
- 90 days of community building before launch day
- Hunter outreach 2 weeks before
- Target 200 upvotes in first 24 hours for top 10
- Go live at 12:01am PST

**PH assets:**
- Tagline: 60 characters, specific outcome, no generic claims
- Description: 260 characters, what it does + who it's for + why now
- Gallery: 4-6 screenshots of product in use
- First comment: Founder's story — why you built this

## Common Rationalizations

| Rationalization | Reality |
|---|---|
| "We'll launch when the product is perfect" | Launch at Pilot stage — users tell you what perfect actually means. |
| "Product Hunt is pay-to-win" | The teams that win PH spent 90 days building relationships before launch day. |
| "We can compress pre-launch to 2 weeks" | At Candidate stage, press lead time alone is 4 weeks. |
